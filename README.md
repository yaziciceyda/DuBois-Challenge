# DuBois-Challenge

The goal of the challenge is to celebrate the data visualization legacy of W.E.B Du Bois by recreating the visualizations from the 1900 Paris Exposition using modern tools.


## 2026

A goal of Du Bois's exhibition in 1900 was to demonstrate the development Black Americans since emancipation in 1865. One hundred and twenty-six years later, in 2026, _this year's challenge will focus on the use of maps and geography_ in the collection. The details of the challenge is [here](https://github.com/ajstarks/dubois-data-portraits/tree/master/challenge/2026).
